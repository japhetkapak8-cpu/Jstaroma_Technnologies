Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Our commitment

Wantok does not tolerate child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE). These Standards apply to all users, content, and activity on Wantok. Wantok is not intended for children below the minimum age required by applicable law to use an online social service.

Prohibited conduct and content

Users must not use Wantok to sexually abuse, exploit, endanger, or target a child. This includes grooming or attempting to groom a child; sexualizing, soliciting, coercing, or extorting a child; arranging or facilitating a child's sexual abuse or exploitation; and encouraging, promoting, or attempting any such conduct.

Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) is strictly prohibited. Users must not create, request, upload, share, distribute, offer, sell, link to, access, or possess CSAM on or through Wantok. This prohibition includes sexualized images or depictions of minors and any material that depicts, promotes, or facilitates child sexual abuse or exploitation.

How to report a concern

Report suspected CSAE, grooming, or CSAM using Wantok's in-app reporting tools. Use the report option on the relevant account, content, or conversation, and include any details that will help us identify and review the concern. Do not download, copy, forward, or otherwise redistribute suspected CSAM, including when making a report.

You can also report a concern by emailing our monitored child-safety contact at wantokchat@gmail.com. Include relevant usernames, links or descriptions of the content, and when you encountered it. Do not attach suspected CSAM.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the relevant child-protection authority.

Review and enforcement

We review reports and assess available account and content information to determine whether our Standards have been violated. We may take appropriate action based on the nature and severity of the concern, including removing prohibited content, restricting content or features, suspending or terminating accounts, and preventing repeat abuse. We may preserve relevant information securely where appropriate or required by law.

Reporting to authorities

Wantok reports suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation to relevant law-enforcement, child-protection, or other authorities when required by applicable law, and may make reports where otherwise appropriate to protect children and comply with legal obligations.

Designated child-safety contact

Wantok's designated child-safety contact is wantokchat@gmail.com. This monitored contact is for child-safety concerns and reports.
