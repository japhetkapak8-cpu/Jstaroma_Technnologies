Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Adult-Only Service

Wantok is an adult-only communication and community service. Users must be at least 18 years old to create an account or use the service.

Minors may not use Wantok, even with permission from a parent or guardian. Accounts reasonably believed to belong to minors may be restricted or removed.

2. Zero Tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

Wantok prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Users must not create, upload, request, share, promote, store, or distribute content that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers a child. Prohibited conduct includes:

  • child sexual abuse material;
  • grooming or sexual solicitation of a child;
  • sexualization of a child;
  • child trafficking;
  • sextortion involving a child;
  • requests for sexual images or content involving a child;
  • attempts to move a child into unsafe or secret communications; and
  • content or conduct that otherwise facilitates child sexual exploitation.

3. Reporting

Users can report suspected child-safety violations through Wantok's in-app reporting tools for conversations, accounts, groups, group members, or content. Reports should include enough information to identify the relevant account, conversation, group, or content. Do not download, copy, forward, or attach suspected CSAM when making a report.

Child-safety concerns can also be reported by email to wantokchat@gmail.com.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement.

4. Review and Enforcement

Wantok reviews child-safety reports and may:

  • immediately remove prohibited content;
  • restrict access to features;
  • suspend or terminate involved accounts;
  • prevent repeat access;
  • preserve information relevant to an investigation; and
  • refer apparent violations to appropriate authorities.

Knowingly false or abusive reports may also result in enforcement.

5. Cooperation with Authorities

Wantok reports apparent child sexual abuse material or exploitation to appropriate child-protection or law-enforcement authorities when required by applicable law.

Wantok responds to valid legal requests and preserves relevant evidence as legally required.

6. Child-Safety Contact

Questions or concerns about Wantok's Child Safety Standards can be sent to wantokchat@gmail.com.
