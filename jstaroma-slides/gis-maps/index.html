01 / GIS PRESENTATIONS

GIS belongs in the presentation, not just the analysis.

Maps often contain the most important information in a GIS project, but presenting that information clearly can require rebuilding layouts manually in PowerPoint.

JStaroma Slides provides presentation structures designed specifically for geographic information, helping you combine maps, findings, interpretation, and supporting information within a consistent slide layout.
