JStaroma Slides

Common questions about the PowerPoint add-in and its planned capabilities.

What is JStaroma Slides?

JStaroma Slides is a Microsoft PowerPoint add-in designed to help users create structured, professional presentations using specialized slide tools and workflows.

Is JStaroma Slides available yet?

JStaroma Slides is currently in development and is being prepared for public distribution.

Does it support GIS presentations?

Yes. GIS and map-focused presentation tools are a major part of JStaroma Slides, including GIS layouts, map frames, findings panels, and additional ArcGIS capabilities under development.

Will there be a Pro version?

A Pro tier is planned for advanced templates, Brand Kits, GIS capabilities, dashboards, AI tools, and cloud-based presentation features.
