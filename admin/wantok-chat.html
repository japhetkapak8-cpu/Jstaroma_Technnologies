Wantok Chat transport records, read through the authorized analytics backend. Unavailable data is never displayed as zero. Management stays disabled until the reviewed transport SQL is installed and enabled.

Transport filters are independent of chat filters.

KPI totals describe current stored records. Active trips require a known future final arrival. Available through-seats sum each approved published trip's minimum remaining capacity across its future boarding segments, not the legacy available_seats column.

Operator Approvals

Every decision requires a reason and is audited server-side. Rejection also cancels the operator's published trips and confirmed reservations using the existing backend workflow. Self-review is prohibited.

Bookings & Seats

Not connected.

Bookings count reservation records; seats reserved sum confirmed passenger quantities. Cancelled and trip-cancelled reservations consume no seats. A seat can be reused on non-overlapping legs, so passenger quantities across a journey can exceed vehicle capacity.

Expand a trip above to inspect each segment. Available seats come only from the backend. Existing transactional reservation functions enforce capacity; this dashboard does not change bookings or request passenger identities.

Transport Analytics

UTC event trends use reservation/operator creation dates and cancellation timestamps. Route, operator, origin/destination, outcome and occupancy charts use trips departing in the selected UTC period. Top breakdowns show up to 20 groups; lists and KPI totals are not date-filtered.

Fares are stored in PGK, but no verified payment ledger was supplied. Revenue is not reported. Occupancy measures confirmed passenger-seat segments divided by capacity-seat segments on non-cancelled trips, not the sum of reservations divided by vehicle capacity.
