Wantok Chat

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Wantok is designed to help users connect with people and communities. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices users have regarding their information.

2. Information We Collect

Wantok may collect information you provide directly, including your name, username, email address, profile information, profile photo, location preferences, group memberships, messages, friend requests, group join requests, and other information you choose to add to your account. Wantok also collects presence information, meaning whether you are currently online in the app, so that other users can see an online (green) or offline (grey) indicator next to your profile photo. Presence is only shared while the app is open and is not shared if you turn off “Show online status” in Privacy & Safety.

3. Account Information

When you create an account, Wantok may store account information such as your name, email address, username, and authentication information. Authentication services may be provided through third-party infrastructure such as Supabase.

4. Location Information

If you enable nearby discovery features, Wantok may use your location to help you find people and groups near you. Wantok should use approximate location for discovery where possible and should not publicly display your precise coordinates to other users.

5. People Nearby

When nearby discovery is enabled, other users may be shown limited profile information about you, such as your display name, username, profile photo, general area, and an approximate distance. Users can send friend requests, which you may accept or reject.

6. Groups and Communities

Wantok may store information about groups you create, join, manage, or request to join. Public groups may allow users to join directly. Private groups may require approval from a group administrator or moderator.

7. Messages and Communications

Wantok may process messages and other communications necessary to provide chat and group communication features. Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information in messages unless necessary.

8. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and maintain accounts, provide messaging and group features, support nearby discovery, process friend and group requests, improve the application, prevent abuse, enforce community standards, and provide user support.

9. Information Sharing

Wantok does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that support the operation of the application, when required by law, to protect users and the platform, or when you choose to make information visible to other users.

10. Public Profile Information

Some profile information may be visible to other Wantok users depending on your privacy settings. This may include your display name, username, profile photo, general location, bio, groups, online status, or other information you choose to make visible. Profile photos may be viewed in an enlarged, full-screen view by other users who can see your profile or chat with you.

11. Friend Requests

Users may send friend requests to other users. The recipient may accept, reject, ignore, or block the request. Wantok may store request status and related account identifiers to provide this feature.

12. Private Group Requests

When you request access to a private group, group administrators or authorized moderators may see information necessary to evaluate your request, such as your name, username, profile information, and request status.

13. Blocking and Safety

Users may be able to block other accounts. When an account is blocked, Wantok may store the relationship between the accounts in order to restrict communication, profile visibility, discovery, or interaction.

14. Data Storage

Wantok may use third-party cloud services to store and process application data. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

15. Data Retention

Wantok may retain information for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain account security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce agreements. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period after deletion.

16. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Wantok account and associated personal information by emailing wanotkchat@gmail.com. Include your username and the email address associated with your account; we may verify account ownership before processing your request. For details about the process and information that may be retained, see our Account Deletion page.

17. Your Privacy Choices

Wantok may provide controls that allow you to manage profile visibility, nearby discovery, location access, friend requests, blocked accounts, group participation, notifications, online status visibility, and other privacy-related preferences. If you turn off online status, you will appear offline to other users.

18. Device Permissions

Wantok may request device permissions such as location, camera, photo library, microphone, or notifications when required for specific features. You can manage these permissions through your device settings.

19. Security

Wantok should use reasonable safeguards such as secure authentication, access controls, database security policies, encrypted network connections, and restricted administrative access to protect user information.

20. Children

Wantok is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required by applicable law to independently use an online social service. Additional age restrictions or parental consent requirements may apply depending on jurisdiction.

21. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Wantok develops new features or legal requirements change. When material changes are made, users should be informed through the application or another appropriate communication method.

22. Contact

Questions, concerns, or requests regarding privacy or your personal information may be sent to wanotkchat@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy is an initial policy draft for the Wantok application. It should be reviewed and updated before public release to reflect the final features, data practices, company details, applicable laws, and app-store requirements.
