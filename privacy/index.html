Wantok Chat

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Wantok is a messaging and community app that helps you stay connected with friends, family and communities. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, which service providers help us run the app, and the choices you have. By using Wantok, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect:

  • (a) account details you provide, such as your name, username, email address, province, bio and profile photo;
  • (b) content you create, such as messages, photos, voice notes, files, stickers, groups, friend requests and reports;
  • (c) your precise location, only when you use People Nearby;
  • (d) a device notification token so we can send you push notifications;
  • (e) basic account information needed to keep the service working, such as when you created your account and last signed in.

We do not access your phone contacts, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

3. Account Information

When you create an account, we store your email address, full name, username and province, plus any profile photo and bio you add. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and Google account ID with us. Your email address is used for signing in, resetting your password and keeping your account secure.

4. Location Information

Wantok only collects your precise location when you open People Nearby in Discover and allow location access. Your location is saved to your account so you can find people near you and they can find you, and it is updated each time you use the feature. Location is optional. You can refuse or remove location permission at any time in your phone settings, and the rest of Wantok will keep working.

5. People Nearby

When you use People Nearby, other Wantok users who also use it may see your public profile in their list of people nearby. Your exact location is never shown to other users.

6. Groups and Communities

When you create or join a group, we store the group name, description, photo, members, member roles and the messages shared in it. Group members can see the member list and messages shared in the group. Public groups may be visible to other users who search for or browse groups.

7. Messages, Calls and Media

We store your messages, photos, voice notes, files and stickers on our servers so they can be delivered to the people you send them to and shown on your devices. Voice and video calls are connected in real time through our calling provider and are not recorded by Wantok. We keep a basic record of each call, such as the call type and time, so it appears in your call history. If you choose to translate a message, the text of that message is sent to our translation provider to create the translation.

8. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • create and manage your account;
  • deliver messages, calls and notifications;
  • show your profile to friends, group members and nearby users;
  • provide features such as translation, stickers and People Nearby;
  • keep Wantok safe by reviewing reports and preventing abuse;
  • answer support requests;
  • fix problems and improve the app.

We do not use your information for advertising.

9. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers. We only share information:

  • with other users, as needed for the features you use (for example, the people you message);
  • with the service providers listed in section 14, who process it on our behalf;
  • when the law requires it or to protect the safety of our users;
  • when you ask us to.

10. Public Profile Information

Your name, username, profile photo, bio and province may be visible to other Wantok users so they can find and recognise you. Do not put information in your profile that you do not want others to see.

11. Friend Requests

When you send a friend request, the other user can see the request and your public profile. We store friend requests and friendships so we can show your friends list and control who can contact you.

12. Private Group Requests

When you ask to join a private group, the group admins can see your request and your public profile. We keep the request until it is approved, declined or cancelled.

13. Blocking, Reports and Safety

You can block other users and report users, messages or groups. When you block someone, we store the block so they cannot contact you. When you send a report or a problem report, we store its details, and any contact email you choose to include, so we can look into it and respond.

14. Service Providers and Data Storage

Wantok uses trusted service providers to run the app:

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and file storage
  • Google Firebase: push notifications
  • Google Sign-In: optional sign-in
  • LiveKit: real-time voice and video calls
  • OpenAI: message translation, only when you choose to translate

These providers process data only to provide their services to Wantok, and may store or process it on servers outside Papua New Guinea.

15. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When we complete a verified deletion request, we delete or anonymize account and personal information associated with your account, subject to information we may retain to meet legal obligations, protect Wantok and its users, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Information remaining in backups may be removed through the normal backup-retention process.

16. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app: go to Settings > Delete account and confirm. If you cannot use the app, email wantokchat@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”. Send the request from the email address associated with your account when possible, and include your Wantok username and registered email address to help us locate your account. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request. Do not include your password or other login credentials. More details are available on our Account Deletion page.

17. Your Privacy Choices

You can edit your profile, choose whether to use People Nearby, manage friend requests, block users, leave groups and control notifications. You can download a copy of your data in Settings, under Privacy Settings, then Download my data. You can also contact us to access, correct or delete your information.

18. Device Permissions

Wantok only asks for permissions when a feature needs them:

  • location (People Nearby)
  • camera (photos and video calls)
  • microphone (voice notes and calls)
  • photos and files (sending media)
  • notifications (message and call alerts)
  • Bluetooth (headsets during calls)

You can change these permissions at any time in your phone settings.

19. Security

We protect your information using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), secure sign-in and database access rules that only let each user see the data they are allowed to see. Messages are encrypted while they are sent and are stored on secure servers, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password and phone safe.

20. Children

Wantok is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at wantokchat@gmail.com and we will delete it.

21. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Wantok changes or when the law requires it. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above, and for important changes we will let you know in the app.

22. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests about your privacy, email us at wantokchat@gmail.com. The latest version of this policy is available at https://jstaromatechnologies.com/privacy/.

Questions about your privacy? Email wantokchat@gmail.com or read the full policy at https://jstaromatechnologies.com/privacy/.
