Account Deletion

Last updated: October 9, 2026

How to request deletion

You can delete your account in Wantok Chat by going to Settings > Delete account and confirming.

If you cannot use the app, request deletion of your account and associated personal information by emailing wantokchat@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”.

To help us locate your account, send the request from the email address associated with your account when possible. Include your Wantok username and the email address registered to the account, and state that you want your account and associated personal information deleted.

Do not include your password or other login credentials. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before acting on a deletion request.

What deletion means

When we complete a verified request, we will delete or anonymize account and personal information associated with your account, subject to the exceptions below. You may lose access to your account and its associated profile, content, and features. Deletion may not remove copies of information that other users already received or saved.

Information we may retain

We may retain limited information when needed to meet legal obligations, protect the security of Wantok and its users, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Information remaining in backups may be removed through the normal backup-retention process.

Questions

For questions about an account or data deletion request, contact wantokchat@gmail.com.
