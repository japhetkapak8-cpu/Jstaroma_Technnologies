Account Deletion

Last updated: 11 November 2026
Version: wantok-legal-2026-11-11-v2

This page covers Wantok Chat (Android package com.wantok.chat) and Wantok Transport. The responsible individual is Japhet Moses Kapak, using the Jstaroma Technologies project/business brand, which is not currently registered or incorporated as a company.

How to request deletion

If your app version provides Settings > Delete account, follow the displayed instructions. The current released control and its complete backend effects have not yet been verified; the email request channel below is available if you cannot find or use it.

If you cannot use the app, request deletion of your account and associated personal information by emailing wantokchat@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”.

To help us locate your account, send the request from the email address associated with your account when possible. Include your Wantok username and the email address registered to the account, and state that you want your account and associated personal information deleted.

Do not include your password or other login credentials. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before acting on a deletion request.

What deletion means

A verified request asks us to delete or anonymize your account and associated personal information, subject to applicable legal exceptions. We may need to clarify its scope and explain which records can be removed or must be retained. Exact automated deletion effects and completion times have not yet been verified, so this page does not promise immediate or complete erasure from every system.

Deletion can remove access to your account, profile and features. Copies already received or saved by others may remain. Ask support to confirm the handling of messages, media, authentication records, application documents, bookings and other records relevant to your request.

Wantok Transport and operator-profile removal

Removing an operator profile is different from deleting your complete Wantok Chat account. Operator-profile removal availability and its effects on vehicles, documents, approval history, public trips and reservations have not yet been verified. State clearly whether you want operator-profile removal, full account deletion, or deletion of particular information.

A deletion request does not automatically cancel a journey or process a refund. Use available cancellation or operator/support channels for upcoming bookings as well. Completed, cancelled, expired or hidden trips and temporary transport messages are not necessarily permanently deleted. No five-day cleanup guarantee is made.

Information we may retain

Limited information may need to remain for a specific legal duty or proportionate security, safety, fraud or dispute purpose. This can affect reservation records, operator decisions and audit history. These purposes do not justify retaining everything indefinitely; ask for an explanation of exceptions applicable to your request.

Specific retention periods, backup rotation and media/document cleanup schedules have not yet been verified. We do not promise a fixed deletion period or that hiding content erases backups or other users' copies.

Questions

For questions about an account or data deletion request, contact wantokchat@gmail.com.
